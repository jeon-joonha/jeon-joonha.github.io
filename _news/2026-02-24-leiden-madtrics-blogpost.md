My first contribution to [Leiden Madtrics](https://www.leidenmadtrics.nl/), the official blog of CWTS, is now live: [**"Reclaiming the scholarly ecosystem: reflections on the first CWTS research seminar of 2026"**](https://www.leidenmadtrics.nl/articles/reclaiming-the-scholarly-ecosystem-reflections-on-the-first-cwts-research-seminar-in-2026).

I must say it is the outcome of what I would call a triple joy:

-   **The joy of reading** a thought-provoking book and listening to the presentation from Samuel Moore, which left me with a bunch of notes.
-   **The joy of discussing** it together with other colleagues at CWTS and those who attended the seminar, who really care about the scholarly ecosystem a lot.
-   **The joy of writing** together with my colleague Tanya Lee, who shares a common background of research with different topics of interest, and not to mention a great experience with the Leiden Madtrics editorial team.

I definitely recommend reading the book *Publishing Beyond the Market*, as we couldn't summarize all its value in this short post. You can also watch the [seminar recording](https://lnkd.in/eD5MjgJ8), and you should come to the next [CWTS Research Seminars](https://lnkd.in/eResAWeh) too!
