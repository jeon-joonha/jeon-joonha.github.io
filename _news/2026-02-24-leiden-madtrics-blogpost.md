My first contribution to [Leiden Madtrics](https://www.leidenmadtrics.nl/), the official blog of CWTS, is now live.

The article, [**"Reclaiming the scholarly ecosystem: reflections on the first CWTS research seminar of 2026"**](https://www.leidenmadtrics.nl/articles/reclaiming-the-scholarly-ecosystem-reflections-on-the-first-cwts-research-seminar-in-2026), reflects on a seminar by Samuel Moore regarding the need to shift Open Access from a commercialized model toward a "commons" approach rooted in community care and scholar-led infrastructure.
