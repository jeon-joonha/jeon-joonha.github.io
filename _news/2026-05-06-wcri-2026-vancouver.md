I recently attended [**WCRI (World Conference on Research Integrity) 2026**](https://wcri2026.org/) in Vancouver, and had the chance to share my work through both an oral talk and a poster (abstract found in the [conference program](https://wcri2026.org/program/)). I enjoyed all the connections and conversations that I made with others in the field who I met.

I also participated in the Doctoral Forum, and to be honest, I'm still a bit surprised (but somehow glad) to share that my work was recognized with the **Excellence in Doctoral Research Award**. It's already been a while but I'll definitely be back soon with a longer piece to reflect more deeply on the experience and what I took away from it. 

Until then, I just wanted to leave a quick update and a quiet thanks to everyone who made the conference a meaningful one.
